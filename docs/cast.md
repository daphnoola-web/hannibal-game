# Cast and clothing — v1 (Chapter 1, New Carthage, 221 BCE)

Written in S2 (2026-10-19). **Evidence for clothing is thin.** Every look below is a **reconstruction** and must carry that label in-game (codex/credits). Ask the user before spending any Meshy credits (S4).

## What the evidence actually supports
| Claim | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Spanish (Iberian) troops wore short tunics edged with purple, "their national dress"; Spanish and Celtic shields were alike; Spanish swords cut and thrust | Polybius 3.114 (Cannae, 216) | Good (ancient, but 5 years later and for soldiers) |
| Gauls fought naked | Polybius 3.114 | Good; not relevant to v1 |
| Libyan/African infantry were armed in Roman fashion with captured arms | Polybius 3.114 | Good, but applies to *after* 217; before that, unknown |
| Iberian warriors typically carried a small shield, spears/javelins and a sword | Osprey-based source via search (weak, summary only) | Medium; **re-check** Quesada Sanz, *Weapons, Warriors and Battles of Ancient Iberia* (2023) |
| Iberian brooches (fibulae) from Baecula (208) can show status/origin | Univ. of Alicante study (search summary only) | Medium |
| Punic art always shows clothed figures; from the 4th c. Greek-style chiton/peplos/himation appear; women with veils on stelae | worldhistory.org "Carthaginian Art" and dealer listings (search summary only) | Weak to medium; funerary context only |
| 3rd-century Punic **civilian male** dress | nothing found | **Unknown.** Use plain tunic and cloak, labelled invented |
| Hannibal's appearance | no reliable portrait (coins debated) | Unknown (already labelled invented) |

**Do not claim** linen, specific colours, helmet types or the caetra/falcata for these characters until a source is read. Where I list them below they are "plausible, unverified".
